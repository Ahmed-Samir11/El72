AI turns a price
into a deal decision.

1 · detect Two-stage anomaly detection

MAD = median(|x − median(x)|)
score = price z vs. MAD
sub-millisecond · catches most drops

Isolation Forest(window)
score > 0.85 → candidate deal
A fast statistical pass catches the obvious movement; the model then checks the price against its recent behavior.

Responsible AI The confidence gate

AI rejects the fake
deal before WhatsApp.

the gate Cross-store + historical context

market min · max · average
spread = (max − min) / min
store rank + gap vs. average

is_historical_low
→ market min near the all-time low
store_historical_lows
→ each store near its own low
El72 compares the whole market and the product's history before it calls a price a deal.

the safeguard Fake-discount detection

reference ≥ 10% above median
AND current < 8% below median
AND competitors ≥ 3% cheaper
AND not near historical min
→ suspicious → −30 penalty
