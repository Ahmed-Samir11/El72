This is the engine room of El72 — the verification that turns a price drop into a deal you can trust.

The problem

Fake discounts are
everywhere.

Bait-and-switch "was" prices, spike-then-drop games, prices that are too good to be true. A naive "it's cheaper than before" check walks right into all of it.

The naive approach

"Just compare
the prices."

Compare today's price to last week's. Problem: last week's price might be fake, the history might be stale, and "cheaper" doesn't mean "a real deal." Naive comparison alerts you to bait.

The El72 approach

Verify against
history.

Every price is checked against a 30-day baseline, with two independent signals that have to agree. If they do, it's a real drop. If a fake-discount pattern shows up, it's flagged before you're ever alerted.

Anomaly detection

Two signals.
Both have to agree.

MAD
Median Absolute Deviation
How far the price is from its robust baseline, in median-deviation units. A z of 14 means 14× the normal noise.
ISOLATION FOREST
Anomaly score
A second, independent lens. 0.97 means "almost certainly not normal." Two signals agreeing is far stronger than one.
Fake-discount detection

Four checks catch
the bait.

1Price below baseline?Is the drop real, or just noise?
2Fake "was" price?Was the old price inflated to fake the discount?
3Spike-then-drop?A temporary spike followed by a "discount" — a classic game.
4Too good to be true?So far below history that it's probably a data error.
