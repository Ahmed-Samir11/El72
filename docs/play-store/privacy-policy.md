# Elhaq — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: v1.0_

Elhaq ("the App") is a price-tracking application for Egyptian grocery
shopping. This policy explains what data the App collects, how it is used,
and your rights.

## Data we collect

- **Account data**: your phone number (used for login via one-time code) and
  your name.
- **Tracked items**: the product URLs/names you add, your target prices, and
  the price history the App fetches from retail websites on your behalf.
- **Device settings**: your chosen theme (light/dark) stored locally on your
  device.

## How we use it

- To authenticate you and keep your session signed in.
- To fetch and store prices for the items you track, notify you when a price
  drops, and show you live deals.

## What we do NOT do

- **No advertising SDKs.** The App contains no third-party ad SDKs and does
  not serve ads.
- **No data sale or sharing.** We do not sell, rent, or share your personal
  data with third parties for marketing.
- **No location tracking.** The App does not access your device location.

## Data retention & deletion

- Tracked items and their price history are stored on our servers while your
  account exists.
- You can delete individual tracked items at any time from the App.
- To delete your entire account and all associated data, contact us at the
  support address below; we will remove your account data within 30 days.

## Security

- Data in transit is encrypted with TLS.
- Access to server data is restricted to our operations team.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
data from children.
