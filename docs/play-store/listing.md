## Long description

### English

Tired of paying full price for groceries? **Elhaq** watches the stores you
shop at and tells you exactly when to buy.

- **Track any product** — add a link, set your target price, and Elhaq
  checks the live price for you.
- **Instant drop alerts** — the moment a price falls below your target, you
  know. No more guessing, no more overpaying.
- **Live deals feed** — see what's on offer right now across tracked stores.
- **Price history** — 90 days of price trends so you can tell a real deal
  from a fake one.

Elhaq is built for Egyptian shoppers: prices in Egyptian Pounds, interface
in Arabic and English, designed for the way you shop.
