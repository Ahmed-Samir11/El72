# Elhaq — Play Data-Safety Form Answers

Reference answers for the Google Play Data safety section (v1.0).

## Data collected

| Data type | Collected? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Phone number | Yes | Account authentication (one-time-code login) |
| Personal info (name) | Yes | Account profile |
| Financial info | No | — |
| Photos/videos | No | — |
| Device identifiers | No | — |
| Location | No | — |
| App interaction data (tracked items, targets) | Yes | Core feature: price tracking & deal alerts |

## Data safety statements

- **Data is deleted when you ask.** Account deletion removes all personal
  and tracked-item data (30-day removal window, see privacy policy).
- **We commit to comply** with the Google Play Families Policy (not
  directed at children).

## Data shared externally

- **Nothing is shared with third parties** for advertising or marketing.
- No ad SDKs are present in the app binary.

## Encryption

- Data in transit: **TLS (HTTPS)** between the app and our API.
- Data at rest: encrypted at the infrastructure level (managed hosting).

## Access & control

- Users can view and delete tracked items in-app.
- Full account deletion available on request.
