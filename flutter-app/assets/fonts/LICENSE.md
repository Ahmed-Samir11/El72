## Note on unused files

The `assets/fonts/` directory also contains the full IBM Plex Sans family
(Condensed, SemiCondensed, variable fonts, and weights not yet used by the app).
These are kept for future milestones; only the files referenced in
`pubspec.yaml` are bundled into the APK.
